Five lessons that came back every few weeks

Lesson Where we met it
The element must suit the physics: low-order elements lock (thin, incompressible), equal-order flow elements violate inf-sup Weeks 1, 7, 8, 9, 11
Stable is not accurate: an energy-stable scheme or a stabilized advection term can be far from the solution Weeks 6, 12, 14
Nonlinear solves need a path: load or parameter continuation, a line search that fits the problem, the reason of every step Weeks 1, 4, 10, 11, 14
The first answer of a converged solver is not yet a result: the converged reason, a mesh study, a balance every week
Parallel bugs are partition bugs: random numbers per rank, unreduced sums, ghost values, arbitrary facet sides, collective destructors Weeks 9, 10, 14, 15

The verification ladder

Rung Question it answers Tool
1 · Exact or manufactured solution is the discretization implemented as derived? predicted vs observed rates, errors against the exact expression
2 · A known limit or benchmark is it the right model? analytical limits, published benchmarks, with a citation
3 · A balance does the discrete solution conserve what the model conserves? consistent fluxes, residual reactions
4 · -np 1 = -np 2 is the code correct in parallel? record, compare_np
5 · Mesh and time-step uncertainty how many digits of the result can I report? observed order, Richardson, GCI

Each rung catches errors the others cannot: a perfect convergence rate (1) of the wrong model fails (2); a matching benchmark on one mesh says nothing about (5).

The LLM workflow, looking back

What the generated scripts in the homework got wrong — and what caught it:

Kind of defect What exposed it
a factor, an exponent or a coefficient in the physics (the script runs, the numbers look plausible) a known answer: an exact solution, a growth rate, a limit
a convention: a sign, a facet side, a velocity or unit definition a symmetry, a balance, a limit with a known value
a partition-dependent construct the same run with -np 1 and -np 2
a claim of “unconditionally stable” or “verified” the reason and the energy at every step, a check that can fail

The pattern: LLM code fails quietly. The defense is a test whose answer you know before you run it — the habit this course asked for in every homework.
