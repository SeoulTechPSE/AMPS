AMPS II · Week 16 — Final Project Presentations and Course Synthesis

There is no final exam and no quiz this week. The session has two parts:

  1. Final project presentations — 12 minutes per student (8 minutes talk + 4 minutes questions, including live questions on your code).
  2. Course synthesis — short slides at the end of the session.
